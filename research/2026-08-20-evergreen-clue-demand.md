## Editorial decision

Publish only Sharp and Light. Both are genuinely ambiguous across grammar and meaning,
can be organized usefully by answer length, and have measurable demand. Sound and Clear
remain candidates for later expansion if Search Console or Bing performance shows actual
site impressions. The new pages group independently written senses and do not reproduce
a publisher grid or historical clue database.
