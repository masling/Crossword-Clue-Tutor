Google search volume, so they are directional prioritization evidence.

The broad query `crossword answer meaning` did not have enough data to show a
trend. Meaning pages should therefore remain selective: publish them for
recurring crosswordese, abbreviations, unfamiliar vocabulary, and idioms where
the page adds durable explanatory value.

## Operating decisions

1. Keep the pattern-aware solver as the high-upside product page, while treating
   the head term as a longer-horizon target because the results are dominated by
   established solver domains.
2. Continue same-day, exact-clue explainers for low-competition discovery.
3. Maintain dedicated NYT Mini and NYT daily clue hubs that aggregate only
   selected, independently explained clues by puzzle date.
4. Prioritize U.S. freshness for NYT content and keep the solver language useful
   to U.K. solvers, who account for most Bing impressions on the solver head term.
