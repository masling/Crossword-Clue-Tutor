Your “Best Sites” collections have long included crossword and word-game tools for English learners. I maintain Crossword Clue Tutor and wanted to suggest a free classroom resource that approaches crosswords from the solving and vocabulary side rather than as another puzzle generator.

The Grades 6–12 routine asks students to identify grammar and clue signals, generate possible meanings, add trusted crossing letters, take a hint before revealing a word, and explain why the final answer fits. It includes differentiation, discussion prompts, an exit ticket, and a printable five-clue worksheet with a separate teacher key.
