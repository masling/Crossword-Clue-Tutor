I maintain Crossword Clue Tutor and would like to suggest a free Grades 6–12 resource for the Colorado Department of Education Media Literacy Resource Bank.

The media-literacy vocabulary pack uses short crossword clues to help students distinguish concepts such as source credibility, bias, evidence, misinformation, privacy, and digital citizenship. Students first identify the clue's grammar and signal words, then use crossing letters and progressive hints before explaining why the final answer fits. The pack includes original reviewed clues, a printable student worksheet, and a separate teacher key.
