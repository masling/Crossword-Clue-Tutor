Your contact page invites ideas for new games, activities, or projects. I maintain Crossword Clue Tutor and would like to suggest a short intermediate-to-advanced ESL vocabulary activity built around crossword clue evidence.

In a 20-minute “Clue Evidence Circle,” small groups underline grammar and clue signals, generate two or three candidate meanings, add trusted crossing letters, take a semantic hint before any answer reveal, and finish by explaining why one word fits better than the alternatives. The activity practices vocabulary, context clues, parts of speech, idioms, and revision when new evidence contradicts a first guess.

The complete free classroom routine is here:
https://crosswordcluetutor.com/guides/crossword-clues-for-vocabulary-learning/

The linked solver requires no student account, and the guide includes differentiation, discussion prompts, an exit ticket, privacy notes, and a downloadable reviewed dataset. You are welcome to test or adapt the activity if it fits ESLActive's resource library.
