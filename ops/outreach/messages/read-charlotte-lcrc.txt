Your FAQ invites PK–12 practitioners to suggest quality resources for the Materials Directory. I maintain Crossword Clue Tutor and would like to suggest a short Grades 6–12 routine that can be delivered as an oral vocabulary and inference activity.

The teacher reads a clue aloud, students paraphrase what they heard, identify grammar and meaning signals, propose two or three candidate words, and explain which evidence supports the final answer. A second round adds trusted crossing letters so students revise when new evidence contradicts a first guess. This makes the activity useful for vocabulary, inference, reasoning, comprehension monitoring, and peer discussion.
