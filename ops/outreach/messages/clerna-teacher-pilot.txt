I maintain Crossword Clue Tutor and would like to submit an original Grades 6–12 lesson plan: “Run a crossword vocabulary pilot in two class sessions.”

Lesson plan: https://crosswordcluetutor.com/educators/crossword-vocabulary-pilot/

The plan is designed for vocabulary, reading, language arts, or a selected content-area class. It uses two 20-minute sessions. Students identify clue grammar, answer length, and meaning evidence; propose two candidates; take a hint before revealing; revise; and explain why the reviewed answer fits. In session two, the teacher reduces one support and students write a clue for a reviewed concept.

The page includes learning goals, materials, preparation, timed teaching steps, assessment prompts, differentiation for multilingual and advanced learners, an extension activity, printable skill packs, a classroom solver, and a structured teacher feedback form. The current library contains 500 original reviewed clues across 29 skill packs. No account is required, and the plan asks teachers not to submit student names or student work.
