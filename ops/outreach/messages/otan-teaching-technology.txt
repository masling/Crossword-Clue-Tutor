I maintain Crossword Clue Tutor and would like to suggest a free lesson-ready resource for OTAN's Teaching with Technology collection.

The resource uses crossword clues to practice vocabulary inference. Learners identify grammar, punctuation, abbreviations, and clue meaning; propose candidate answers; add trusted crossing letters; request a hint; and explain the evidence for the final word. The 20-minute guide includes supports for emerging learners, extensions for advanced learners, assessment prompts, and a printable worksheet with original clues and a separate instructor key.

Teacher guide:
https://crosswordcluetutor.com/guides/crossword-clues-for-vocabulary-learning/

Printable worksheet:
https://crosswordcluetutor.com/educators/crossword-vocabulary-worksheet/

Classroom solver:
https://crosswordcluetutor.com/classroom-solver/

It can support ABE, ESL, and high-school-equivalency vocabulary work. No learner account is required, clue matching stays in the browser, and the classroom pages omit Google Analytics. The materials do not copy a commercial grid or complete answer key.
